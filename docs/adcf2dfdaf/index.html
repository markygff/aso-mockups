What we do

Electrical services for homes and businesses

We take on urgent faults, planned installs and compliance work. Pick the job that fits and get in touch.

Emergency Electrician

For power loss, burning smells, buzzing consumer units, sparking outlets or breakers that keep tripping. Call us and we'll make it safe.

House Rewire

Full or partial rewires for older homes with worn or unsafe wiring. The work is tested and certified when it's done.

Solar PV Installation & Storage

Solar panels and battery storage fitted to your home, so you can use more of the power you generate.

Solar Installation Condition Reports

A check on an existing solar system to see whether it's safe and working as it should.

Commercial Electrical Projects

Electrical work for offices, shops and schools, including refurbishments and planned projects.

Electrical Compliance

Testing and reports that help businesses and landlords show their electrics meet the rules.

Commercial EV Charger Installation

EV charge points fitted at your workplace for staff, visitors or company vehicles.

LED Lighting Upgrades

Old lighting swapped for LED fittings in commercial premises. A good time to do it is during a refit.

Questions

Common questions about our electricians

Q.Do you have an emergency electrician in Bristol?

Yes. We handle urgent faults such as total or partial power loss, burning smells, sparking sockets and breakers that keep tripping. Phone lines are open 08:00-16:00 and 17:00-21:00 Monday to Friday, and 08:00-21:00 at weekends.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We cover Bristol, Bath, Swindon, South Wales and Gloucestershire, for both homes and businesses. If you're not sure you're in our area, give us a call and ask.

Q.Do you give a quote before starting work?

Yes. We give you an upfront quote and simple options before any work begins, so you know the cost first.

Q.Are your electricians NICEIC approved?

Yes. We're NICEIC approved, our electricians are 18th Edition qualified and fully insured, and we also hold CHAS, ECA and Buy With Confidence.

Q.Will I get a certificate for the work?

Yes. Testing, documentation and certification come as standard on notifiable work such as rewires and consumer unit changes.
