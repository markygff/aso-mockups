What we do

Fire safety and electrical services

We cover fire safety and lighting under one roof. Tell us what the building needs and we'll sort it.

Fire Risk Assessment

If you're responsible for a building, you have a legal duty to reduce the risk of fire. We carry out the assessment properly and take that pressure off you.

Fire Doors

Supply, installation and upkeep of fire doors for flats, offices, hospitals and schools. Each door has to be the right spec and fitted to the required standard.

Fire Alarm Systems

Design, supply, installation, commissioning and maintenance, from single zone conventional systems to multi-network analogue addressable systems.

Emergency Lighting

Emergency lighting installed and tested so people can get out safely if the power fails. For any building that needs to meet the current standard.

Emergency Lighting Design

We plan emergency lighting for your building from the start, so the layout meets the standard before anything goes on the wall.

Mains Lighting Design

Design, supply and installation of mains lighting for commercial buildings. Useful for a refit, a new layout or a move away from old fittings.

Buildings Lighting Design

Lighting design for the whole building, planned alongside your emergency lighting so the two work together.

Questions

Fire safety questions we get asked

Q.Is there a 24 hour fire alarm engineer in Peterborough?

Yes. We run a 24 hour call out. Ring us, tell us what's happened and we'll talk you through what we can do and when we can get to you.

Q.Do I legally need a fire risk assessment for my building?

If you're responsible for a building, reducing the risk of fire is your legal duty. A fire risk assessment is how you show it's been done. We can carry out the assessment for you and take that pressure off.

Q.What fire alarm systems do you install?

We design, supply, install, commission and maintain fire alarm systems. That runs from single zone conventional systems up to multi-network analogue addressable systems.

Q.Are you up to date with the new emergency lighting standard BS 5266-1:2025?

Yes. We've published guidance on the changes to BS 5266-1:2025, and to BS 5839-1:2025 for fire alarms. Call us if you want to know what the changes mean for your building.

Q.Do you give quotes for fire door and fire alarm work?

Yes. Send us the quote form or give us a call with details of the building and the work you need. We'll talk it through with you and explain what happens next.
