What we do

Plumbing and heating services

From burst pipes and blocked toilets to boiler breakdowns, we handle planned and emergency work. All of it is fully guaranteed.

Plumbing

Blocked pipes, water leaks, hot water cylinders, ball valves and overflow systems. We deal with all aspects of plumbing.

Blocked Sinks and Toilets

A blocked sink or toilet can't wait. We're on hand 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to clear it quickly and with minimum fuss.

Central Heating

Cold radiators or no heating at all? Heating problems need a fast response, and help is always at hand.

Boiler Servicing and Repairs

Regular servicing keeps your boiler running well. If it breaks down, call our emergency helpline for a speedy fix.

Power Flushing

Heating slow to warm up? A power flush can cut the risk of breakdowns, extend the life of your system and help keep your bills down.

Leaking radiator valve

A leak at the radiator valve can damage floors and ceilings below. Call us and we'll sort the valve and stop the leak.

Tap change and fix leak

A tap that won't stop leaking wastes water and gets worse over time. We'll fix the leak or fit a new tap.

Questions

Common questions

Q.Is there an emergency plumber available at night or at weekends?

Yes. Our emergency helpline is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can call us at night and at weekends.

Q.How quickly can a plumber come out?

We don't publish a set response time. Call the 24 hour helpline, tell us what's wrong and where you are, and we'll tell you when an engineer can get to you.

Q.Do you charge a call out fee?

No. There's no call out charge and we work to a fixed fee. Ask us to explain the cost of the job when you call so you know where you stand.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We cover East Sussex, Hampshire, Kent, London, Surrey and West Sussex. Call us if you're not sure whether we reach your address.

Q.Are you Gas Safe registered?

We hold Gas Safe accreditation. We're also accredited with CHAS, ECA and RECC, listed on Checkatrade and a Which? Trusted Trader.
