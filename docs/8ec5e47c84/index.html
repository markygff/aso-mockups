What we do

Solar installation services

We help homes and businesses save money on energy, cut carbon and rely less on the grid. Pick the work you need below.

Domestic Solar

Solar panels for your home, sized to your roof and how you use electricity. For anyone who wants lower bills.

Commercial Solar

Solar panels for business premises. For firms that want to bring down energy costs and cut their carbon.

Agricultural

Solar for farms and agricultural buildings. Big roofs on sheds and barns put to work making power.

Batteries

Battery storage that keeps the power your panels make so you can use it in the evening. We're a Duracell Approved Installer.

EV Charging

Home and business chargers for electric vehicles. Pair one with solar to charge your car from your own roof.

Off-Grid

Off-grid systems for properties that need their own power supply away from the mains.

Solar Thermal

Solar panels that heat your hot water. A good fit if you want to cut what you spend heating water.

Maintenance

Upkeep for solar systems, including panel cleaning. For anyone whose system isn't performing the way it should.

Questions

Solar panel questions answered

Q.Do you give free solar panel quotes in Northern Ireland?

Yes. We offer a free survey and quote. We look at your property and how you use energy, then recommend the system that suits you.

Q.Which areas of Northern Ireland do you cover?

We're based in Newtownards and we install across all of Northern Ireland. That includes Belfast, Lisburn, Hillsborough, Moira, Newry, South Down, Armagh, Derry, the North West, Fermanagh and Enniskillen.

Q.How long does a solar panel installation take?

Many of our installs are done in a single day. Larger systems can take longer. One customer review mentions a 2 day install. We'll tell you what to expect for your job when we quote.

Q.Are you an MCS certified solar installer?

Yes. We're an MCS registered installer. We're also registered with NICEIC and RECC, and we're a Duracell Approved Installer for batteries.

Q.Can I add a battery or EV charger to my solar panels?

Yes. We fit batteries so you can store the power your panels make, and we install EV chargers for homes and businesses. We can fit them with a new system or alongside panels you already have.
