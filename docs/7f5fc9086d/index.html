What we do

Gas, Heating and Pipework Services

We take on domestic and industrial gas work, servicing and pipework. Here's what we do.

Gas Boiler Installation

A new system or combination boiler, fitted to Gas Safe requirements. We'll advise on the right boiler for your home.

Gas Boiler Servicing

A thorough annual service to current regulations. You can opt in for a yearly reminder so your boiler keeps running safely.

Gas Fire Installation

All types of gas fire fitted, including conventional flue, balanced flue and flueless fires. Ask us for advice first.

Gas Fire Servicing

An annual gas fire service done to current regulations, with an optional yearly reminder so it keeps working safely.

Log & pellet burner servicing

Servicing for log and pellet burning stoves and fires. We're HETAS registered for solid fuel work.

Pellet stove servicing

Regular servicing for pellet stoves so they keep burning cleanly and safely through the year.

Industrial Boilers

Industrial heating supplied, installed and serviced. We aren't tied to one manufacturer, so we can supply any brand.

Pipework, plant room design & Installation

Plant room design, layout and upgrades, district heating networks, and industrial pipework installation and modification.
