What we do

Electrical and solar services in Kent

We handle solar, electrical and servicing work for homes and businesses. Every job is tailored to the property and the people using it.

Solar PV

Solar panels designed around your property, your energy use and your future plans. For homeowners who want to generate their own electricity.

Commercial solar

Rooftop solar for businesses and organisations. Turn unused roof space into an asset and cut operating costs as energy prices rise.

Electrical work

Domestic and commercial electrical work, from upgrades to full rewires, carried out by an NICEIC Approved Contractor.

Full and partial rewires

For older wiring or a renovation that needs new circuits. We'll rewire the whole property or just the part that needs it.

Lighting and additional sockets

More sockets where you need them and new lighting for homes, plus commercial lighting such as warehouse LED high-bay lights.

Electrical reports and testing

Testing and reports on your electrics. Useful when you're buying, selling or letting a property, or just want to know the wiring is safe.

Service & Maintenance

Solar servicing, fault finding, cleaning, bird protection and system upgrades. For panels that aren't producing what they should.
