Questions

CCTV questions answered

Q.How quickly can you come out to fix my CCTV?

We offer a rapid response for faulty cameras, storage issues and connection problems. Evening and weekend slots are available too. Send us the details through the quote form and we'll come back to you with the first available slot.

Q.Can you upgrade my old CCTV system?

Yes. We modernise existing equipment with 4K resolution and remote smartphone monitoring. Tell us what you've got now in the quote form and we'll suggest what's worth keeping and what's worth replacing.

Q.Can I watch my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Yes. Remote smartphone monitoring is part of our upgrades and installs, so you can check your cameras from wherever you are.

Q.Do you install CCTV for homes as well as businesses?

Yes. We supply and install high-definition CCTV for homes and businesses. Jobs can be big or small, and each system is tailored to your property and what you need to cover.

Q.Do you maintain the network as well as the cameras?

Yes. We provide CCTV and network maintenance, so the cameras, the recorder and the connection between them are all looked after together.
