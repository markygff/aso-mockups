Questions

Spa day questions

Q.How much is a spa day at Beauty Cocktail Spa in Horsham?

Weekday spa days, Monday to Thursday, are £175.00 per person. Friday, Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays are £195.00 per person. Both include £50.00 towards any treatment from the list, minimum 6 ladies. The Luxury Spa Day is £230.00 per person for 2 or more. All prices include VAT.

Q.Is Beauty Cocktail Spa only for women?

Yes. The spa is only for ladies. You also get exclusive use of the facilities for your group, so you won't be mixing with other guests during your day.

Q.Can I book a single treatment without a spa day?

We don't offer individual treatments on their own. You can have a treatment when you book one of our spa day packages. Call us and we'll help you pick the right one.

Q.How many people can come on a spa day?

Our group spa days are for a minimum of 6 and a maximum of 14 ladies. If there are fewer of you, the Luxury Spa Day is available for 2 or more ladies.

Q.What should I bring and what time does a spa day start?

Arrival is 9.30am and departure is 6.00pm. Robes and towels are provided. Please bring flip-flops or slippers to use in the spa, and let us know about any dietary requests before you come.
