Questions

Solar panel questions, answered

Q.Do you give free quotes for solar panels in Nottingham?

Yes. We start with a free site survey. We look at your property and then send you a tailored proposal for a solar PV or electrical system designed to get the most from your energy savings.

Q.Which areas do you cover for solar panel installation?

We're based in Nottingham and install for customers across the UK. Get in touch with where you are and we'll let you know.

Q.How long does a solar panel installation take?

It depends on the property and the system. One customer told us their whole process took 2 weeks from start to finish. We'll give you a clear timeline once we've surveyed your property.

Q.Are your solar panel installers MCS certified?

Yes. We're MCS certified and install in line with MCS and UK regulations. We're also registered with TrustMark, NAPIT, RECC, ECA, CHAS and Constructionline.

We give you a full system handover and certification, and we stay on hand with ongoing support. We can also show you how to keep an eye on your system through its app.
