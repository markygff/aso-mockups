What we do

Our electrical services

We do electrical work for homes and businesses, from a single circuit to a full new build. Here's what we cover.

Residential Electrical Services

Electrical work for your home, from small jobs to full installations. We work closely with you from design through to the finished job.

Commercial Electrical Services

Installations, load calculations, circuit design, 3 phase distribution, emergency lighting, cable containment and general maintenance for businesses.

Extensions and Renovations

Re-wires, extensions and smart home retrofits, including programmable lighting controls. For minor or major renovation work.

Electrical loading calculations and advice

Load calculations and straight advice at the planning stage, so a new build or big project gets the right electrics from the start.

Internal and external lighting design

Lighting planned for inside and outside your property, including energy-efficient fittings with timers and automated controls.

Maintenance

Testing and inspecting for homes and businesses. Regular checks are required by law and keep your property safe.

Solar PV

Initial design and energy analysis, then installation and commissioning. All work is done to G98/99 approval standards.

Solar PV & Energy Storage

We look at how you use electricity and advise on solar and energy storage that make sense for your home or business.

Questions

Common questions about our electrical work

Q.Do you cover all of Bedfordshire?

We work for homes and businesses across Bedfordshire. If you're not sure we cover your address, call us or send the quote form and we'll let you know.

Q.How quickly can an electrician come out?

Call us and tell us what's happened. We'll talk it through with you and arrange a visit as soon as we can.

Q.How do I get a quote for electrical work?

Send the quote form with details of the job, or give us a call. We'll come back to you to talk it through and price the work.

Q.Is electrical testing and inspection a legal requirement?

Regular recommended testing and inspecting is required by law and it protects your property. We specialise in testing and inspecting for homes and businesses and keep up to date with the latest legislation.

We start with initial design and an energy analysis of your property. Then we install and commission the system. All of it is done to G98/99 approval standards.
