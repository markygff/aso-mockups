What we do

Our solar services in Stroud and the Cotswolds

We cover solar PV, battery storage and commercial work. Tell us what you need and we'll design a system that fits your roof and your usage.

Solar PV

Roof mounted solar panels for homes and businesses, designed around your roof, your shading and how much power you actually use.

Solar PV and Battery Storage

Panels and a battery fitted together, so the power you generate in the day is there to use in the evening instead of going back to the grid.

Solar Battery Backup Systems

Battery storage that keeps power available when you need it. Good if you already have panels or you want cover when the grid goes down.

Commercial installations

Solar for business premises, including EV charging on commercial projects. We handle the design and the install from survey to sign off.

Solar Solutions

Questions

Common questions about solar in Stroud

Q.Do you give free quotes for solar panels?

Yes. Call us and we'll arrange a free home survey. We look at the roof, talk through solar PV, battery storage and what suits the property, then quote from that.

Q.What areas do you cover for solar installation?

We're based in Stroud and cover the surrounding area. If you're not sure whether you're in range, ring us or email and we'll tell you.

Q.Are you MCS accredited?

Yes. We're MCS accredited for MIS 3002 solar photovoltaics and MIS 3012 battery storage classes 1, 2 and 3. We're also NAPIT registered, membership number 76874, and members of RECC.

Q.Can I add a battery to solar panels I already have?

Yes. We fit solar battery backup systems as well as full solar PV and battery storage packages. We'll check what you've already got on the survey and tell you what can be added.

Q.What happens on the first visit?

It's a free home survey. We look at your roof, your shading and how you use electricity, then talk through the options. You get a written quote afterwards and no pressure to go ahead.
