What we do

Electrical and green energy services in London

We cover the whole job, from the first energy audit through to installation and the maintenance after it. Here's what we do.

Residential Green Energy Installation

Green energy and battery kit fitted at home, whether that's a new build or an older property that needs bringing up to date.

Commercial Green Energy Solutions

Green energy work for offices, hotels, clinics and cold storage sites that can't afford to lose power.

Green Energy Battery Systems

Battery storage that holds power for when you need it, so you're covered when the grid drops out.

Energy Audits

We go through how your building uses energy and where it's being wasted, then tell you what's worth changing.

Maintainance Works

We service, monitor and repair the system once it's in, so small faults get sorted before they stop the power.

Fast 1-3 Day Installation

Most installs are set up to run over 1 to 3 days. Ask us what that means for your job before you book anything in.
