What we do

Our electrical and green energy work

We cover green energy installation, battery storage, audits and maintenance for homes and businesses in London. Here's what we do.

Residential Green Energy Installation

Green energy fitted at home, from first survey to commissioning. Good if you want lower bills and power that holds up when the grid drops.

Commercial Green Energy Solutions

Green energy for offices, clinics, hotels, cold storage and other sites that can't afford to go dark during the working day.

Green Energy Battery Systems

Battery storage sized to your building, so you keep power during an outage and use less from the grid at peak times.

Energy Audits

We look at how your building uses power, where it's wasted and what a change would actually save you before you spend anything.

Maintainance Works

Servicing and repairs on installed systems, so batteries, controls and switching keep working the way they should.

Fast 1-3 Day Installation

Most installs are done in one to three days, so your home or site is back to normal quickly.

Questions

Common questions

Q.How quickly can you install a green energy or battery system?

Most installations take one to three days on site. Timings depend on the survey and the system you need, so call or send the form and we'll tell you what yours involves.

Q.What areas of London do you cover?

We cover London and we're based in Ruislip. If you're outside London, call us and we'll tell you honestly whether we can take the job.

Q.Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Fill in the form and we'll design a power plan for your property and come back to you with a free consultation. There's no charge for the conversation.

Q.Are you certified?

Yes. Ecosmart Power Solutions Ltd is certified to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, certificate number 522892026, certified by Citation ISO Certification Limited.

Q.What happens on the first visit?

We look at how your building uses power and what you need it to do. That's the energy audit. From there we design a system for your building and explain what it covers.
