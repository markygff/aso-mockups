What we do

Plumbing and heating services

From a new boiler to a new bathroom, here's what we do. Every job is guaranteed.

Boiler Upgrades

We swap an old or unreliable boiler for a new one. Worth looking at when repairs keep coming back or your bills keep climbing.

Central Heating

New central heating systems and changes to the one you've got. Systems start from £1,750.

Boiler Servicing

A proper check of your boiler so it runs safely and efficiently. Best booked before the cold weather sets in.

Common questions

Q.How much does a new central heating system cost?

Central heating systems start from £1,750. The final price depends on your home and the system you need, so we'll give you a free quote before any work starts.

Q.Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Quotes are free and so is advice. Send the form below with a few details of the job and we'll come back to you with a quote.
