What we do

Electrical services in Nottingham

We look after homes, landlords, letting agents and businesses. Small repairs, full rewires and everything in between.

Emergency electrician services and fault finding

Power failures, tripping circuits, burning smells, faulty sockets and flickering lights. We find the fault and make it safe, day or night.

Domestic electrical installations and repairs

Electrical work around the home, from a small repair or extra sockets to bigger installation jobs.

Commercial electrical installation

Installation and ongoing maintenance for shops, offices and other commercial buildings.

Full and partial property rewiring

Old or unsafe wiring replaced, either throughout the property or in the parts that need it.

Electrical Installation Condition Reports

A full check of the fixed wiring in a property. Useful for landlords, letting agents, buyers and anyone unsure of their installation.

Consumer unit and fuse board upgrades

An old fuse board replaced with a modern consumer unit. Worth doing if yours is dated or keeps tripping.

Electrical inspection and testing

Inspection and testing of electrical installations in homes and commercial premises, carried out to the relevant British Standards.

Lighting, sockets and switch installations

New lights, extra sockets and replacement switches, fitted neatly and safely.
