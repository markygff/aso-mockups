What we do

Aesthetic treatments in Exeter

Every treatment starts with a proper conversation about your skin and the result you want. Ana then tailors the treatment to you.

Dermal Fillers

Filler treatments, including lips, planned around your face to give natural, balanced results rather than an overdone look.

Skin Boosters

Injectable treatments for skin that looks tired or dull and needs more hydration and quality.

Polynucleotides

An injectable skin treatment for people who want to improve the overall look and condition of their skin.

Mesotherapy

A course of small injections into the skin or scalp, including scalp mesotherapy. Results build over the sessions.

Microneedling

A skin treatment for texture and overall skin quality, suited to people who want a gradual improvement.

Fat Dissolving Injections

Injections aimed at small, stubborn areas of fat. Ana will talk through whether it suits you at consultation.

Chemical Peels

Professional peels for skin that needs a refresh. The right peel is chosen for your skin at consultation.

Skin Tag Removal

Removal of skin tags you'd rather not have, carried out in a private, professional clinic.

Questions

Questions about aesthetic treatments in Exeter

Q.Who carries out treatments at Æterna Aesthetics in Exeter?

Every treatment is carried out by Ana Gouveia, the founder. She's a qualified Aesthetic Nurse Practitioner and NMC-registered nurse with over a decade of healthcare experience and an advanced qualification in Facial Aesthetics.

Q.Do I need a consultation before a prescription-only treatment?

Yes. Any prescription-based procedure needs a face-to-face consultation with Dr Miranda, the clinic's GP prescriber, beforehand. The consultation fee for this appointment is £30. It makes sure the treatment is safe and right for you.

Q.Is the clinic properly qualified and safe?

Ana is an NMC-registered nurse and a member of the ACE Group, which puts patient safety, ethical practice and evidence-based treatment first. Prescription-only treatments are overseen by a fully qualified GP prescriber in line with UK clinical standards.

Q.How do I book an aesthetic treatment in Exeter?

You can book online or send an enquiry. The enquiry button opens WhatsApp so you can message the clinic directly about the treatment you're interested in.

Q.Will my results look natural?

Ana's focus is natural, balanced results that suit your face. She takes the time to understand what you want before any treatment and won't rush you.
