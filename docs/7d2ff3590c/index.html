Questions

Solar PV and EV Charging Questions

Q.Do you install solar panels on houses in Airdrie?

Yes. We design and fit domestic solar PV systems for homes in Airdrie. Our in-house design team plans the system around your property, and our own installers and scaffolders carry out the work.

Q.What happens at the first consultation?

We start by finding out what you need and what you want the system to do. From there our in-house design team works up the right setup for your home or business, and we talk you through our recommendations.

Q.Can I get solar panels with no upfront cost?

Our Power Purchase Agreement lets you switch to solar without a large upfront cost. It's a practical way for homes and businesses to get renewable power and cut energy costs without a big outlay. Send us a quote request and we'll explain how it works for your property.

Q.Do you fit EV chargers at home and for businesses?

Yes. We install home EV charge points, and commercial EV charging for fleets, staff and customers. We can fit charging alongside a solar PV system or on its own.

Q.Do you look after solar panels after they're installed?

Yes. System maintenance is part of what we do. We want your system to keep giving you the performance it was designed for long after handover.
