What we do

Renewable heating and solar services in Glasgow

We fit and look after renewable energy systems for homes across Scotland. Every job starts with a proper look at your property.

Solar PV

Panels on your roof that generate your own clean electricity, so you rely less on the grid. Worth a look if your bills keep climbing.

Solar + Storage

Solar panels paired with a home battery. You store the energy you generate and use it when you want, day or night.

Air Source Heat Pump

A low-carbon upgrade for your home heating. Grants of up to £9,000 are available through Home Energy Scotland.

Underfloor Heating

A low-maintenance, energy-efficient way to get consistent warmth throughout your home.

Insulation

Modern insulation that keeps as much of your heat in as possible, so less of what you pay for escapes.

Servicing & Maintenance

Regular servicing to keep your heat pump working properly. We support you after the installation is done.

Questions about solar and heat pumps in Glasgow

Q.Do you offer free surveys for solar panels in Glasgow?

Yes. Every job starts with a free, no-obligation survey. We look at your property, talk through what you want to achieve and recommend the system that suits it.

Q.Can I get a grant for an air source heat pump in Scotland?

Grants of up to £9,000 are available through Home Energy Scotland for air source heat pumps. Ask us about it when you book your survey and we'll talk you through it.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We're based in Glasgow and install renewable energy systems across Scotland. Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm whether we can help.
