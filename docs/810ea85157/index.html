Questions

Plumbing and heating questions

Q.Do you do emergency plumbing repairs in Bournemouth?

Yes. Our plumbing and heating service covers emergency repairs as well as servicing and installs. Our team is available Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Outside those hours, send the form and we'll get back to you.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We're based in Bournemouth and work across Dorset, including Poole, Christchurch, Weymouth and Dorchester. We also cover Hampshire, including Southampton, Portsmouth, Winchester, Basingstoke and Andover, and London.

Q.Do you give free quotes?

Yes. We start with a free, no-obligation consultation. After a site survey you get a clear, fixed-price quote with the full specification and project programme.

Q.What happens on the first visit?

Our team visits your property, looks at the site and talks through what you need. We then suggest the most efficient and sustainable way to do the job before we quote.

Q.Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Our engineers are Gas Safe accredited, and we also hold MCS, CHAS, SafeContractor, ECA and SELECT accreditation. Every job is commissioned, tested and certified at handover.
