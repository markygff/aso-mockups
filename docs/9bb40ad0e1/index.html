What we do

Steel fabrication services

We take on fabrication work of all sizes, from structural steel to gates and railings. Tell us what you need and we'll tell you how we'd make it.

Steel Fabrications

Fabricated steelwork made to your drawings or your brief. Call us when you need a part, a frame or a full job made in steel.

Structural steelwork - beams and columns

Steel beams and columns for building and alteration work. Talk to us when your builder or engineer has specified structural steel.

In-house CNC tube/pipe Bending

Tube and pipe bending done on our own CNC kit, so the work stays in one place. Ideal for rails, frames and bespoke bent sections.

Machining, Milling & turning

Machining, milling and turning for parts that need to be made or finished to a precise size. Send us your part or drawing.

Automated Gates

Automated gates for homes and commercial sites. A good fit when you want secure access without getting out of the car.

Wrought Iron Gates

Wrought iron gates made to suit your entrance. Ask us when you want a gate that looks right on a period or traditional property.

Railings

Railings for boundaries, steps, landings and drops. Call us when you need an edge made safe or a boundary tidied up.

Glass balustrades

Glass balustrades for stairs, landings and balconies. A clean option when you want protection at an edge without blocking the view.
