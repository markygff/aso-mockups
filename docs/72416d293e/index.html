What we do

Electrical and heating services

We take on single jobs and full projects for homes and businesses. Here's what we install and look after.

Single or three-phase power installations

New supplies and upgrades for homes, workshops and commercial units. Three-phase suits heavy machinery and larger premises.

Distribution systems

Boards and distribution that get power safely to every part of a building. You'll need this for a new build, an extension or a refit.

Conduit installation

Conduit to protect cabling in plant rooms, warehouses, garages and anywhere cables are open to knocks and damage.

Power circuitry design and installation

We plan and fit the circuits your building needs, from a new kitchen to a full commercial fit-out.

Trunking systems installation

Tidy, accessible cable routes for offices, shops and industrial units. A good fit where the wiring will change over time.

Lighting systems design and installation

Lighting designed around how the space is used, then fitted to regulations. For homes, offices, shops and industrial units.

Heating systems design and installation

We design and fit heating systems for houses and commercial buildings, sized for the space you need to keep warm.

Gas boiler installation & servicing

New boilers fitted and existing ones serviced by our Gas Safe engineers. A regular service keeps your boiler safe and working well.

Questions

Common questions

Q.Which areas do you cover in Greater Manchester?

We work across Greater Manchester for homes and businesses. For commercial work we operate nationwide, and our team is equipped to take on projects overseas too.

Q.Are you Gas Safe and NICEIC registered?

Yes. We're Gas Safe and NICEIC approved. We also hold CHAS, Safe Contractor, ECA, Avetta and RECC accreditation, and our electricians hold City & Guilds qualifications.

Q.How quickly can an electrician come out?

Send us the details using the form on this page and tell us how urgent it is. We'll look at what's needed and come back to you about when we can get to you.

Q.How do I get a quote for electrical or heating work?

Fill in the quote form with what you need and where the job is. Every job starts with a consultation, where we work out exactly what you need so the quote fits the work.

Q.What happens on the first visit?

Every project begins with a consultation. Our team looks at the job and talks through what you need. After that we can design, fit and commission the work, or just handle the part you want.
