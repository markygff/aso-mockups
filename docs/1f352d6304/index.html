Q.Do I need interlinked smoke alarms in Scotland?

Yes. Since February 2022 all homes in Scotland must have interlinked fire alarms, so if one goes off they all go off. Homeowners are responsible, and landlords are responsible for rented homes. We give free quotes for interlinked smoke alarms.

Q.Can you repair my existing burglar alarm?
