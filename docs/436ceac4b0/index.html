Questions

Heat pump questions we get asked

Q.Do you give free heat pump quotes in Epworth?

Yes. Quotes are free. Fill in the quote form with a few details about your property and what you're after, and we'll get back to you.

Q.Can you help if my heat pump has stopped working properly?

Yes. We offer troubleshooting and fault-finding on existing installations, along with ongoing technical support. Describe the fault on the form so we know what we're dealing with before we come out.

Q.What happens on the first visit for a heat pump?

We start with a consultation to understand your property, your needs and your budget. We'll match that to suitable products and explain the likely savings, costs and incentives.

Q.Are you an MCS accredited heat pump installer?

Yes. We're accredited with MCS and RECC, and we design systems up to MIS-3005. We also hold CHAS and Avetta.

Q.Can you help me apply for a heat pump grant?

Yes. We'll advise on the grants and incentives that apply to your project and help with the application.
