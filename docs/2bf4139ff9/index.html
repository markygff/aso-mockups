Questions

Common questions

Q.What areas do you cover in Dorset?

We work across Dorset, on commercial, domestic and marine jobs. If you're not sure whether your job is near enough to us, ring and ask before you go any further.

Q.Do you make balustrades and staircases to order?

Yes. Balustrades, staircases and railings are one of the main things we make. We can work in mild steel, stainless steel or aluminium, and you can see finished balustrade work in our photo gallery.

Q.What metals do you weld?

Ferrous and non-ferrous materials, including aluminium, mild steel and stainless steel. Tell us what the job is and where it's going, and we'll say which metal makes sense.

Q.Do you take on sub-contract fabrication for builders?

Yes, we undertake sub-contract work, and we're fully insured. Send us the drawings or a description of what you need made and we'll come back to you with a price.

Q.How quickly can you get a price to me?

We don't publish a turnaround time, so the honest answer is to ask. Ring us or send the form with what you need, and we'll tell you when we can look at it and what it'll cost.
