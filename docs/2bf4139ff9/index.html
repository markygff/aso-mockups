Questions

Common questions

Q.What areas do you cover for steel fabrication?

We cover Dorset. If you're near the edge of it, give us a ring with the job and the postcode and we'll tell you whether we can get to you.

Q.Do you make balustrades and staircases to measure?

Yes. Balustrades, staircases and railings are made to your sizes in mild steel, stainless steel or aluminium, then fitted.

Q.What metals do you work with?

Ferrous and non-ferrous, including aluminium, mild steel and stainless steel. Tell us what the job is and we'll advise on the metal.

Q.Do you take on sub-contract work for builders?

Yes, we undertake sub-contract work. Send the drawings or the sizes and we'll tell you if it's something we can take on.

Q.How much does a steel balustrade or staircase cost?

There's no set price, because every job is a different size and metal. Send us the measurements and what you want it to look like, and we'll come back with a price.
