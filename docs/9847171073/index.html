What we do

Our fabrication and welding services

We cover the whole job, from design through fabrication to the finish. Everything is bespoke, so tell us what you're after and we'll work out the detail with you.

Structural steel work

Beams, columns and frames fabricated to your drawings. For new builds, extensions and openings where the load has to be carried on steel.

Portal frame buildings & cladding work

Portal frames and cladding for workshops, stores and agricultural or industrial units where you need a large clear span.

Gates, railings & ornamental ironwork

Bespoke gates, railings and decorative ironwork for homes and yards, made to fit your opening rather than off the shelf.

Guard Railing & Barriers

Guard railing and barriers for edges, walkways, car parks and loading areas where people or vehicles need protecting.

General Fabrication & Welding Services

One-off brackets, frames, repairs and awkward metalwork nobody stocks. If you can describe it or sketch it, we can usually make it.

Stainless Steel & Aluminium Fabrication

Stainless and aluminium work for kitchens, food areas, marine and anywhere mild steel would rust or look wrong.

MIG, TIG & ARC Welding

MIG, TIG and ARC welding on mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium, in the workshop or on your site.

Galvanising, Powder Coating

Galvanising, powder coating and wet spray finishing so the steel goes up already protected and in the colour you want.

Questions

Common questions

Q.Do you give free quotes for metal fabrication?

Yes. We offer a free consultation and quotation, whatever you need. Send us your drawings, measurements or even a sketch and we'll come back to you with a price and no obligation.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We work across Devon from our workshop in Plympton. If you're not sure whether you're in reach, ring us with your postcode and we'll tell you.

Q.Can you weld on site rather than in the workshop?

Yes. We do onsite welding and steel erection, and we have our own fitting team, so we can weld and install at your property or site rather than only in the workshop.

Q.Do you make one-off gates and railings, or only standard sizes?

All our work is bespoke. Gates, railings and ornamental ironwork are made to your opening and your design, so nothing has to be cut down or made to fit a stock size.

Q.When can I speak to somebody about a job?

Our opening times are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Call or email during those hours, or leave your details on the quote form and we'll get back to you.
