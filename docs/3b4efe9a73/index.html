Questions

Commercial solar questions, answered

Q.Do you offer a free solar assessment for businesses?

Yes. Send us five details and one of our engineers will review your site and come back to you within one working day. There's no obligation, and if the numbers don't work for your site, we'll say so.

Q.How long does a commercial solar installation take?

Most projects take a matter of weeks from first enquiry to a system generating on your roof. The install itself is around two weeks, done by our own electricians and planned around your working day.

Q.What is the payback on commercial solar panels?

It depends on your site and how you use power. In our published case studies, a furniture manufacturer in Sutton saw a 3.6-year payback and a retirement village in Corsham a 3.5-year payback. We'll give you realistic figures for your own building.

Q.Do you install commercial solar outside Nottingham?

Yes. We're based in Nottingham and work across the East Midlands, and we deliver commercial solar projects UK-wide.

Q.Do you handle the DNO application and MCS certification?

Yes. We handle the entire process, including feasibility, DNO grid applications, MCS certification, building control and commissioning. We're MCS Certified and our background is construction and project management.
