Questions

Solar and electrical questions answered

Q.Do you do emergency solar or electrical call-outs in Dorset?

Our team is available 24/7. Tell us what's happening when you get in touch and we'll give you clear advice on what to do next.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We work across Dorset, for homes and businesses throughout the county.

Q.Do you give free quotes for solar panels?

Advice from our team costs nothing. Once we understand what you need, we give you a clear, upfront estimate with no hidden costs.

Q.Will solar panels work during a power cut?

Standard systems shut off during an outage for safety. With battery backup added, you can still have power when the grid goes down.

Q.What maintenance do solar panels need?

Very little. Occasional cleaning and routine checks are enough to keep them running efficiently. If output drops, our inspection and repair service will find out why.
