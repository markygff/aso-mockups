What we do

Our electrical services

We look after homes, landlords and commercial property. Here's what we do.

EICR & Testing

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for landlords, homeowners and commercial property managers. Includes periodic inspections and fault finding.

Domestic Installations

Consumer unit upgrades and electrical work around the home. Give us a call if your fuse box is old or your power keeps tripping.

Full & Partial Rewires

Full or partial rewires for older properties where the wiring is tired, unsafe or can't keep up with how you use the house.

Smart Home & Lighting

Smart lighting and home control, set up to suit your mood, help with security and cut wasted energy.

EV Charger Installation

Home and business EV charging points fitted by certified installers. Worth sorting if you've got an electric car or plan to get one.

Solar PV & Battery Storage

Solar panels and battery storage fitted to help you cut your energy bills as costs keep rising.

Commercial & Industrial

Large-scale electrical installations for commercial property developers, including three-phase work.

Contract EICR Testing

Large-scale EICR testing for property developers, estate managers and landlords with a portfolio to keep compliant.

Common questions

Q.Do you do emergency electrical repairs in Leicester?

Yes. Emergency repairs are available and we run a 24/7 priority line. Call the emergency number on this page and tell us what's happened so we can advise you straight away.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We cover Leicester, Warwick and the wider Midlands. We also take on national contracts for property developers and estate managers. If you're not sure we cover you, give us a call and ask.

Q.Do you give free quotes?
