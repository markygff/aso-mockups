What we do

Commercial rooftop solar services

We handle the full job, from first survey to long-term maintenance. Every system is designed around your building and your energy use.

Commercial Rooftop Solar

Solar PV for warehouses, industrial units, schools and public sector buildings. Flat, pitched and standing-seam metal roofs, from 10kW to 1MW+.

Battery Storage

Commercial battery systems from 10kWh upward. Use your solar power in the evening, cut peak tariff costs or keep backup power on site.

Maintenance & Monitoring

Real-time monitoring, annual health checks, thermal imaging surveys and O&M contracts. Fault response is typically same or next day.

MCS-certified installation

Every install is MCS-registered, so you can export unused power through the Smart Export Guarantee. We also manage the DNO application and grid connection.

Questions

Commercial solar questions

Q.Do you offer free site surveys for commercial solar?

Yes. We visit your site, assess your roof and energy usage, and give you a fully detailed proposal. The survey is free and there's no obligation.

Q.What areas do you cover for commercial solar installation?

We're based in Hampshire and install commercial rooftop solar across Hampshire, West Sussex and the South Coast.

Q.What size of commercial solar system can you install?

We install systems from 10kW to 1MW+ on flat, pitched and standing-seam metal roofs. Standing-seam roofs can be clamp-fixed with no penetrations. Structural and load calculations are included.

Q.How long does it take to get commercial solar installed?

There are four steps: free site survey, custom design, installation and switch on. It's typically weeks from survey to a live system, and we keep disruption to your operations to a minimum.

Q.What happens if my commercial solar system develops a fault?

Every installation comes with real-time monitoring as standard. Our fault response is typically same or next day, and we offer annual health checks and O&M contracts for larger systems.
